# Data and results

The [ACDC Challenge](https://www.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/Challenge/acdc/databases.html) hosts the original training and test releases. The included download scripts retrieve the corresponding releases from the official CREATIS server and save the source terms. The repository contains research code and aggregate outcomes. Obtain images and masks from the original source for non-commercial scientific research under the [dataset terms](https://humanheart-project.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/database/api/v1/item/66e290e0961576b1bad4ee3c/download) and [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Cite O. Bernard et al., “Deep Learning Techniques for Automatic MRI Cardiac Multi-structures Segmentation and Diagnosis: Is the Problem Solved?”, IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 37(11), 2514–2525, 2018. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2018.2837502)

`results/annotation-evidence-aggregate.json` holds patient-aggregated outcome estimates and resampling intervals. `results/annotation-target-audit.json` counts whether selected slice–class nominations matched their reference masks by class, phase and state. The corresponding scripts reconstruct summaries from local evaluation output. The audit estimates annotation effort in masks rather than clinician time.

`results/acquisition-baseline-aggregate.json` contains post-hoc uniform-random and pooled-image-difference controls at the frozen 160-mask budget and their paired contrasts against balanced targeting on the ACDC test cohort.

The external transfer audit uses the original Sunnybrook 2009 cine MRI release from Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre, Toronto. The [Cardiac Atlas Project data page](https://www.cardiacatlas.org/sunnybrook-cardiac-data/) specifies CC0 1.0. The converter downloads the [original challenge archives](https://sourceforge.net/projects/cardiac-mr/files/) and records their SHA-256 checksums locally. Cite Radau et al., Evaluation Framework for Algorithms Segmenting Short Axis Cardiac MRI, The MIDAS Journal, 2009. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.54294/g80ruo)

`results/sunnybrook-transfer.json` contains the external LV-cavity evaluation at end-diastole and end-systole. `results/sunnybrook-myo-ed.json` contains the secondary end-diastolic LV myocardium evaluation. The cohort contains 45 patients with 805 contoured LV cavity slices, of which 420 have paired epicardial contours.

The York external cohort consists of 33 short-axis cine MRI examinations acquired at the Hospital for Sick Children, Toronto. The original laboratory release permits free research use and asks that users cite Andreopoulos and Tsotsos, *Efficient and generalizable statistical models of shape and appearance for analysis of cardiac MRI*, Medical Image Analysis 12(3), 335–357, 2008. The preprocessing script retrieves the original image, contour and spacing files from York University’s data host and verifies pinned SHA-256 hashes. [Lab download instructions and terms](https://web.archive.org/web/20260104121914id_/http://jtl.lassonde.yorku.ca/software/datasets/) · [York archive host](https://data.nvision.eecs.yorku.ca/datasets/MRI/) · [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2007.12.003)

York annotations cover slices on which both LV boundaries were visible. Each patient’s end-diastolic and end-systolic phase estimates are the largest and smallest summed endocardial polygon areas on the same slices with contours in all 20 frames. Scoring uses the 265 positive contoured slices at estimated end-diastole and the 233 at estimated end-systole. `results/york-lv.json` and `results/york-myo.json` report separate cavity and myocardium assessments. York is a pediatric cohort and its phase labels are estimates from the contours.

Both external cohorts have no RV reference. Uncontoured slice-class pairs have unknown status. Recall is conditional on manually contoured positive regions and uses the original ACDC thresholds. The `acdc-*-reference.json` files score corresponding positive slices of the 50 official ACDC test patients. The repository contains aggregate reports only.
